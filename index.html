Gentle veterinary care for Honeydew families

Routine visits, preventative care, urgent guidance, and practical pet advice for dogs, cats, birds, reptiles, and small companions.

A local clinic experience built around clear answers, calmer pets, and owners who know what happens next.

Consultations & Checkups

Book a general health visit, new pet check, or follow-up when something feels off and you want a professional eye.

Preventative Care

Vaccinations, parasite prevention, dental advice, nutrition support, and wellness planning for every life stage.

Care for more than cats and dogs

Small Pets

Health checks and handling guidance for rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, rats, mice, and other small mammals.

Large Animal Guidance

Practical support and referral direction when your animal needs care beyond a standard clinic visit.

Exotics & Birds

Initial advice for rabbits, birds, reptiles, and unusual pets, with referral recommendations when specialist care is needed.

When to get in touch

Something Changed

Loss of appetite, coughing, limping, vomiting, skin irritation, behaviour changes, or a pet that simply seems unlike themselves.

Planning Ahead

Use appointments for vaccinations, sterilisation advice, senior wellness, travel preparation, and long-term health planning.
