How booking works

Use the form below for routine or non-critical requests. If your pet is struggling to breathe, collapsing, bleeding heavily, or in severe pain, phone immediately instead of waiting for an online reply.

Step 1: Tell us about the pet

Include species, age if known, and the reason for the appointment.

Step 2: Share the main concern

Symptoms, timing, appetite, behaviour changes, and urgency help the clinic prioritise properly.

Step 3: Prepare for the visit

Bring previous vaccination records, medication names, photos or videos of symptoms, and any recent diet changes.

Step 4: Confirm the appointment

The form prepares an email draft and stores a local copy in your browser so the request is not lost.
